Who I Am

Rashka Barre is a Somali content creator focused on storytelling, documentaries, travel, experiences, culture and original video content.

The work here is built around real places, real people and real moments — told with a cinematic eye and a respect for the stories being shared.

What I Make

This store hosts original documentaries, experiences and other video content created by Rashka Barre. Each video is designed to be watched properly — not scrolled past.

Short previews are available so you can see the tone and quality before you buy. The full videos are delivered after purchase.
